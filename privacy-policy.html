Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how G1RL ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information about you when you use G1RL.

Information we collect

G1RL is a calculator app designed to be private by default. We collect the absolute minimum information needed to operate the app:

  • No account required. G1RL does not require you to sign in or create an account. We do not collect your name, email, or any personal identifier.
  • Calculation history. Your calculation history is stored locally on your device only. It is never transmitted to us or any third party.
  • App preferences. Your theme, key shape, font, and other settings are stored locally on your device.
  • Purchase status. If you purchase the G1RL Pro Pass or any individual theme/shape, RevenueCat (our purchase management partner) receives your anonymous purchase status to unlock the corresponding features. RevenueCat receives a randomly generated user identifier — never your real identity, name, or payment details.
  • App Store / Google Play data. When you make a purchase, Apple or Google handles the payment. We never see or store your payment card number, billing address, or Apple ID / Google Account credentials.

What we do NOT collect

To be explicit:

  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No location data
  • No contacts, photos, or device files
  • No crash reports tied to your identity

How we use information

The minimal information we receive (purchase status from RevenueCat) is used solely to:

  • Unlock premium themes and key shapes you have purchased
  • Restore previous purchases when you reinstall the app or use a new device with the same Apple ID / Google Account

Information sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. The only third parties involved in operating G1RL are:

  • Apple App Store and Google Play — to process purchases (governed by their own privacy policies)
  • RevenueCat — for subscription/purchase management (no personally identifiable information is shared)

Your rights

Because G1RL stores your data locally and does not link it to your identity, you can:

  • Delete all your data instantly by deleting the app from your device
  • Restore previous purchases at any time via the "Restore Purchases" button on the paywall (uses your Apple ID / Google Account, not us)
  • Contact us at miguel.zom.mg@gmail.com with any privacy questions or to request information about purchases tied to your Apple ID / Google Account

Data security

Because we don't collect personally identifiable information, there is very little to secure on our end. Purchase status data handled by RevenueCat is encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-standard practices.

Children's privacy

G1RL is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the effective date at the top of this policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at miguel.zom.mg@gmail.com.
